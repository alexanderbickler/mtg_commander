How the rating works: the hard rules set the lowest bracket the deck can be in (the rules floor). Tutors, fast mana and free interaction can push the recommendation up one bracket when the power is clearly higher than the rules alone show. The Game Changers list matches the February 2026 update (53 cards). Mass land denial, extra-turn, combo and tutor detection uses built-in lists of well-known cards, so it may miss obscure ones. Always talk it over with your playgroup before the game.
